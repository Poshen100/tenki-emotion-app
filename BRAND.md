## 3. Brand Tone

TENKI must always feel:

| Tone Pillar | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Calm** | Not anxious, not urgent, not pushy |
| **Scientific** | Grounded in real physiology and data |
| **Pivotal** | Carries a sense of turning-point power |

**Avoid**:
- Too therapeutic / spa-like / gentle wellness
- Too niche / trader-jargon heavy
- Generic AI copy ("unlock your potential", "empower your journey")

## 4. Positioning

TENKI is not a trading app. TENKI is not a meditation app.

TENKI is a **state-regulation platform** — a tool that helps users identify, regulate, and learn from shifts in their mental and physiological state.

> "把轉機變成可訓練、可量化的能力。"

This positioning is **one layer above** both trading apps and meditation apps. It appeals to:
- Traders who want decision discipline
- Professionals who want to manage peak performance
- Anyone who wants to understand and regulate their own emotional baseline

---

## 5. Dopamine Baseline Model (Brand Language)

TENKI's core product insight is the three-state dopamine model. Copy must reflect this without making clinical claims.

| State | User Experience | TENKI Language |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| **Above Baseline** | Overstimulated, FOMO, impulsive, reward-chasing | "身體訊號顯示過度活躍狀態" |
| **At Baseline** | Clear, grounded, ready for decisions | "你的身體回到穩定基準線" |
| **Below Baseline** | Withdrawal, fatigue, craving, low motivation | "身體正在發出休息訊號" |

**Never claim** precise dopamine measurement. Always use relative, observational language.
